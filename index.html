🎙️ AI Voice Tools — Try These

Free ways to turn text into speech — some just read it in a ready‑made voice, some can copy a voice from a short clip.

How to use this: Click Open on any card — it takes you to a free web demo (nothing to install). Type some text, press generate, and listen or download the audio. Look for a demo marked Browser — those run in a web page. Clone means it can imitate a specific voice from a short sample.

Kokoro

BrowserClone: No

Fast, natural, lots of built‑in voices. The easiest “just read this nicely” option — great for narration.

Open demo →

Chatterbox

BrowserClone: Yes

Top‑rated open voice tool (beat ElevenLabs in blind tests). Give it ~10 seconds of a voice and it speaks in that voice.

Open demo →

edge‑tts (Microsoft voices)

BrowserClone: No

Hundreds of ready‑made voices in many accents and languages. Instant and clean — no copying voices, just lots of choices.

Open demo →

F5‑TTS

BrowserClone: Yes

Quick, high‑quality voice cloning from a short clip. A strong step up if Chatterbox isn’t quite right.

Open demo →

XTTS v2 (Coqui)

BrowserClone: Yes

Well‑known voice cloner that works across many languages. A reliable all‑rounder.

Open demo →

Dia (Nari Labs)

BrowserClone: Yes

Built for two people talking — write a little script and it voices a back‑and‑forth conversation.

Open demo →

Bark

BrowserClone: No

The playful one — can laugh, sigh, and add emotion. Fun for expressive or character voices.

Open demo →

Fish / OpenAudio

BrowserClone: Yes

Very natural, often called the closest to ElevenLabs. Good when you want polish.

Open demo →
